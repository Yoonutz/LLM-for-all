## Torn (2004 video game) - Wikipedia
> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torn_(2004_video_game)

Torn is a text-based MMORPG developed by Joe Chedburn, launched in 2004. The game centers around a virtual world involving crime and business, where players advance by engaging in various criminal activities and player-versus-player combat. Torn features an in-game currency and offers a unique experience point system that differs from typical MMORPGs. A notable update, Crimes 2.0, was implemented in June 2023, enhancing the game's crime mechanics and offering players more complex criminal acts and benefits.

## Torn (2018 video game) - Wikipedia
> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torn_(2018_video_game)

Torn is a virtual reality sci-fi adventure game developed by Aspyr Media, released on August 28, 2018. The game features a first-person view and is available on various platforms including Windows, PlayStation VR, and Oculus VR. Torn has received moderate reviews, with a Metacritic score of 73/100 for PC and 65/100 for PS4, and has been nominated for several awards for its technical achievements and soundtrack.
